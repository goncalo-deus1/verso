# Living in Alcochete: what you need to know before choosing this area

**Quick summary:** Alcochete is the least populated municipality in the AML, with only 19,000 residents, and grew 9% over the last decade. The median T2 rent sits between €750 and €900. It is the municipality directly affected by the future Luís de Camões airport, with construction scheduled for 2034 to 2037 on the Campo de Tiro de Alcochete. A good choice for those looking for a quiet riverside setting 10 minutes from Parque das Nações via the Ponte Vasco da Gama. Less suited to those who prefer areas with little predictable urban change over the coming years.

*Updated on 30 April 2026.*

---

## Is Alcochete worth living in?

Alcochete has a well-preserved riverside character, with a historic town, salt pans and proximity to the Tejo Estuary Nature Reserve. The town is compact and has lively traditional retail.

The big question for the next 10 to 15 years is the impact of the new airport. The preventive measures within the 15 km radius are already in force (DR February 2026) and the municipality will change substantially, with job creation, new infrastructure and increased housing pressure.

## How much does it cost to rent a T2 in Alcochete in 2026?

The median rent for a T2 in Alcochete sits between €750 and €900 per month. The most expensive areas are Alcochete-Vila and the riverfront. The most affordable are São Francisco and Samouco.

### Three-year T2 rent projection (2026 to 2029)

Base scenario: accumulated growth between 18% and 30%, taking the median T2 rent to between €900 and €1,150 per month by 2029. Alcochete is probably the AML municipality with the greatest appreciation potential because of the new airport. The start of airport construction will create housing pressure from workers linked to the project. The preventive measures within the 15 km radius, in force since February 2026, may reduce new supply in some areas, accentuating the rise.

## Population and density

Alcochete had 19,148 residents in the 2021 Census, 9% more than in 2011, one of the largest relative increases in the AML. The area is 128.36 km², with a density of 149 inhabitants per km², the lowest in Greater Lisboa and Southern Setúbal after Mafra.

The population is growing mostly in Alcochete-Vila and in São Francisco.

## Who lives in Alcochete? Resident profile

Alcochete has an established middle-class profile alongside new residents who arrived over the past 10 to 15 years, drawn by quality of life and proximity to Parque das Nações. There is a community of qualified professionals working in eastern Lisboa.

The international presence is low but may grow significantly over the coming years because of the airport.

## Transport and connection to the centre of Lisboa

Alcochete has a clear advantage: it is 10 minutes from Parque das Nações via the Ponte Vasco da Gama.

By car via the Ponte Vasco da Gama, access to eastern Lisboa takes 10 to 15 minutes outside peak hours. To central Lisboa, 25 to 35 minutes.

TST buses provide regular links to Lisboa (Sete Rios, Cais do Sodré).

No train or direct ferry: the closest river crossing is the Montijo ferry. The closest rail link is Pinhal Novo (Palmela).

The Third Tagus Crossing, announced in 2024 alongside the airport, will link Lisboa-Chelas to Barreiro. The indirect impact on Alcochete is positive, especially if there are improvements to complementary access routes.

## Planned urban projects in the municipality

**Luís de Camões airport** — `Announced`
To be built on the Campo de Tiro de Alcochete, with an area of around 2,500 hectares and capacity for 45 million passengers. Completion is scheduled for 2034–2037, with investment of around €9 billion. The current Humberto Delgado Airport in Lisboa will be closed.

**Parque Cidades do Tejo (remaining land)** — `Announced`
The Campo de Tiro land not occupied by the airport falls under Parque Cidades do Tejo, S.A. (Decree-Law of September 2025), to be developed with housing and amenities.

The preventive measures within the 15 km radius have been in force since February 2026, covering 71,000 hectares. All new planning operations in part of the territory are subject to prior verification by ANAC.

Alcochete has 462 families on the waiting list, a high number considering the size of the municipality.

The Freeport Outlet, in São Francisco, remains one of the largest retail destinations on the south bank.

## Schools, healthcare and retail

In education, public coverage is good. Private schools are mostly in Alcochete-Vila. Higher education is in Lisboa.

In healthcare, the Hospital do Barreiro-Montijo is the public reference. Health centres cover the essentials.

In retail, the Freeport Outlet is the main hub. Traditional retail is strong in Alcochete-Vila.

## FAQ

### Is Alcochete good for families?
Yes. Alcochete-Vila and São Francisco have good schools, parks and a quiet residential setting. T3 rent between €900 and €1,100.

### Is it worth buying a home in Alcochete before the airport?
For long horizons (10 to 15 years), it makes sense. The appreciation potential is high because of the airport and the TTT. The risk is the urban change and noise associated with the airport after 2034.

### Will airport noise affect Alcochete-Vila?
The town lies outside the airport's footprint (which is on the Campo de Tiro, further north). Noise impact depends on the final approach and take-off routes. Environmental simulations are under way.

### Which are the cheapest areas in Alcochete?
Samouco and the inland areas of São Francisco have T2 rents between €650 and €800.

### Is it worth living in Alcochete if I work in eastern Lisboa?
Yes. It is one of the best south-bank options for Parque das Nações in terms of travel time (10 to 15 minutes via the Ponte Vasco da Gama).
