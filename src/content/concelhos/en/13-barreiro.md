# Living in Barreiro: what you need to know before choosing this area

**Quick summary:** Barreiro is one of the municipalities in the Lisbon Metropolitan Area with the best price-quality-journey-time ratio. It has 73,000 residents and a median T2 rent between €650 and €800. It has a direct ferry to Lisboa (Barreiro-Terreiro do Paço). It is the arrival point of the future Third Tagus Crossing, a project that could substantially change its appeal. A good choice for those who want to pay much less than in Lisboa and use the ferry. Less suitable for those who work in the western part of Lisboa without using the 25 de Abril Bridge.

*Updated on 30 April 2026.*

---

## Is Barreiro worth living in?

Barreiro has a reputation as a decaying industrial area, but the reality is changing. The riverfront has been rehabilitated, there is a cultural life of its own (Iminente Festival, Auditório Municipal Augusto Cabrita), and prices remain significantly lower than in Lisboa, Almada or Seixal.

The big future opportunity is the Third Tagus Crossing (TTT), which will link Lisboa-Chelas to Barreiro with road and rail components.

## How much does it cost to rent a T2 in Barreiro in 2026?

The median T2 rent in Barreiro is between €650 and €800 per month. It is one of the lowest figures in the Lisbon Metropolitan Area.

The most expensive areas are Lavradio (next to the riverfront) and the rehabilitated historic centre. The most affordable are Coina, Santo André and inland areas of Alto do Seixalinho.

### Three-year T2 rent projection (2026 to 2029)

Base scenario: accumulated growth between 15% and 25%, taking the median T2 rent to between €800 and €950 per month by 2029. Barreiro is one of the municipalities with the greatest potential for value appreciation in the Lisbon Metropolitan Area because of the Third Tagus Crossing. The closer construction of the rail and road bridge gets, the greater the housing pressure. Areas near the future railway station (Lavradio) may record even larger increases.

## Population and density

Barreiro had 73,044 residents in the 2021 Census, 0.5% less than in 2011. The area is 36.39 km², with a density of about 2,008 inhabitants per km².

Population loss is mild but has been stabilising in recent years. The densest parishes are Barreiro-Vila and Lavradio.

## Who lives in Barreiro? Resident profile

Barreiro has an established working-class profile, with a strong industrial tradition linked to the former CUF (Companhia União Fabril). The population is mostly Portuguese, with a significant presence of Cape Verdean and Brazilian communities in some areas.

In recent years, young creatives and professionals from Lisboa have been moving to Barreiro for the price-ferry-culture combination. The riverfront has been attracting private investment and cultural initiatives.

## Transport and connection to central Lisboa

Barreiro has three main options:

**Third Tagus Crossing** — `Announced`
New bridge from Lisboa-Chelas to Barreiro with road and rail components. Barreiro is the main beneficiary of this link on the south bank. No confirmed construction start date.

Ferry (Barreiro-Terreiro do Paço): around 25 minutes crossing, frequency of 30 minutes during rush hour. It is the most used connection by Barreiro residents working in Lisboa.

Fertagus train via Coina (station on the border of Barreiro with Palmela). From Coina to Pragal it takes around 15 minutes. From Coina to Roma-Areeiro it takes around 30 minutes.

Car via the 25 de Abril Bridge: access to Lisboa in 15 to 20 minutes outside rush hour. During rush hour, the time increases significantly.

## Planned urban projects in the municipality

**Quimiparque / Arco Ribeirinho Sul** — `Announced`
The former CUF industrial land (Quimiparque) is one of the largest land reserves in the Lisbon Metropolitan Area. It is part of the Arco Ribeirinho Sul, now under the remit of Parque Cidades do Tejo, S.A. under the Decree-Law of September 2025.

Barreiro has 812 families on the waiting list. The City Council is executing Recovery and Resilience Plan projects.

The rehabilitation of the riverfront continues, with investment in public space and cultural amenities.

## Schools, healthcare and retail

In education, public provision is good, with several primary and secondary schools. There is no higher education of its own, with university students travelling to Lisboa or Almada (Universidade Nova).

In healthcare, Barreiro-Montijo Hospital is the public reference and also serves Montijo. Private provision is limited.

In retail, Barreiro Retail Park, Modelo Continente and the traditional commerce of Lavradio cover the offering. For larger shopping, the usual trip is to Almada (Almada Forum) or Setúbal.

## FAQ

### Is Barreiro good for families?
Yes. Lavradio, Santo André and Alto do Seixalinho have good schools, parks and a residential environment. T3 rent between €800 and €1,000.

### Is it worth buying a house in Barreiro before the TTT?
Analysts consider Barreiro one of the markets with the greatest potential for value appreciation in the Lisbon Metropolitan Area, depending on when the TTT moves forward. The risk is delay or cancellation of the project.

### Is Barreiro worth it if I work in Lisboa?
Yes, especially if you can use the ferry. From Barreiro-Vila to Terreiro do Paço is 25 minutes by ferry. From Coina to Roma-Areeiro is 30 minutes by train.

### Barreiro or Seixal: which is better?
Seixal is more residential and has more green spaces. Barreiro is more urban, with a cultural life of its own and greater value-appreciation potential (TTT). Seixal for tranquillity, Barreiro for urbanity and appreciation potential.

### What is the Quimiparque?
The Quimiparque is the former CUF industrial zone (Companhia União Fabril) in Barreiro. At more than 250 hectares, it is one of the largest land reserves in the Lisbon Metropolitan Area. It has been under the remit of Parque Cidades do Tejo since September 2025.
