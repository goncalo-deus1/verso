# Living in Oeiras: what you need to know before choosing this area

**Quick summary:** Oeiras is the third most expensive municipality in the Lisbon Metropolitan Area, with a median T2 (2-bedroom flat) rent between €1,300 and €1,400. It has 172,000 residents and the highest purchasing power per capita in the country. It concentrates the offices of technology and pharmaceutical companies (TagusPark, Lagoas Park, Quinta da Fonte) and has the best road connection to Lisboa via the A5 and the Marginal. A good choice for qualified professionals working at multinationals based in these business parks. Less suitable for those looking for a beach nearby other than Carcavelos, or for budgets below €1,000.

*Updated on 30 April 2026.*

---

## Is Oeiras worth living in?

Oeiras competes with Cascais for the upper-class profile of the western shore, but with one practical difference: it is 15 to 25 minutes from Lisboa by train or by car outside peak hours. The combination of strong salaries (an average above neighbouring municipalities), careful urban planning and proximity to employers makes it attractive to qualified professionals.

Paço de Arcos, Caxias, Algés, Linda-a-Velha and Carnaxide are the most sought-after parishes to live in. Oeiras-Vila and Porto Salvo have the strongest recent growth.

## How much does it cost to rent a T2 in Oeiras in 2026?

The median rent per square metre in Oeiras is €13.80, according to INE (Q4 2024). For an 80 m² T2, the rent sits between €1,300 and €1,400 per month. Values advertised online in Imovirtual's January 2026 Barometer indicate an average rent of €1,400, with a year-on-year rise of 12%.

The most expensive areas are Paço de Arcos, Algés (along the riverfront), Quinta da Fonte and Carnaxide. The most affordable are Barcarena and the inland areas of Porto Salvo.

### Three-year T2 rent projection (2026 to 2029)

Base scenario: accumulated growth between 8% and 14%, bringing the median T2 rent to between €1,400 and €1,500 per month in 2029. Oeiras has three factors that sustain demand: the highest purchasing power per capita in the country, a strong municipal focus on science and technology (TagusPark, INL Oeiras), and the planned long-term connection via the Red Line metro to Alcântara. The pessimistic scenario is stabilisation if new supply on the Quinta da Fonte and Outurela plots offsets demand.

## Population and density

Oeiras had 171,802 residents in the 2021 Census, 0.2% fewer than in 2011, one of the few negative variations in the Lisbon Metropolitan Area. The area is 45.88 km², with a density of 3,744 inhabitants per km², the fourth highest in the Lisbon Metropolitan Area.

The population is ageing in established areas (Paço de Arcos, Algés) and growing in new areas (Outurela, Porto Salvo, Tercena).

## Who lives in Oeiras? Resident profile

Oeiras has the most corporate-business profile on the north shore. Middle and senior staff at tech companies (Microsoft Portugal, Vodafone, Bosch), pharmaceuticals (Roche, Bayer, Pfizer) and consultancies are concentrated here. The international presence is strong in Carnaxide and Quinta da Beloura (on the Cascais side).

The upper-middle-class Portuguese population is dominant in the established parishes. Families with children prefer Algés, Paço de Arcos and Linda-a-Velha for the schools and proximity to the river.

## Transport and connection to central Lisboa

The Cascais-Cais do Sodré rail line crosses Oeiras with several stops (Algés, Cruz Quebrada, Caxias, Paço de Arcos, Santo Amaro de Oeiras, Oeiras). From Oeiras to Cais do Sodré is about 25 minutes. From Algés to Cais do Sodré is 12 minutes. Frequency is 20 minutes at peak hours.

By car, the A5 and the Marginal are the two options. The A5 is faster outside peak hours; the Marginal is more scenic. Both have predictable congestion at peak hours.

Light surface metro between Oeiras and Alcântara, with interchange to the Red Line metro. Under study, no confirmed operation date.

The business parks (TagusPark, Lagoas Park) have their own and shared shuttle transport for employees.

## Planned urban projects in the municipality

New office buildings continue to enter the business-park area around Quinta da Fonte and adjacent axes, attracting housing demand.

Oeiras has 1,467 families on the waiting list. The City Council has been investing in cost-controlled housing, with several projects underway, including acquiring private units to integrate into the public stock.

The regeneration of the riverfront between Algés and Pedrouços continues, in partnership with Lisboa.

## Schools, healthcare and retail

Oeiras has a good public and private school offering. Within the municipality, the Externato Padre António Vieira (Linda-a-Velha), Colégio da Torre (Paço de Arcos) and the public school clusters of Carnaxide and Paço de Arcos anchor the network for families with school-age children. The IST TagusPark, in Porto Salvo, is the secondary campus of the Instituto Superior Técnico and the only university presence within the municipality. For reference international education, most Oeiras families travel to Carcavelos (St Julian's, in the neighbouring municipality of Cascais) or to Linhó (Carlucci American School, in Sintra).

In healthcare, the Hospital da Luz Oeiras and the Hospital CUF Tejo (just over the border in Lisboa) serve the municipality. The Hospital de São Francisco Xavier (Lisboa) is the public reference.

In retail, Oeiras Parque, Alegro Alfragide (on the border with Amadora) and the Cascais Villa Shopping Centre concentrate the offering. Traditional retail is stronger in Algés and Paço de Arcos.

## FAQ

### Is Oeiras good for families with children?
Very much so. Algés, Paço de Arcos and Linda-a-Velha have good schools, proximity to the river and parks (Parque dos Poetas, Jardim Municipal). T3 rents start at €1,500.

### Is Oeiras worth living in if I work at Saldanha?
Yes. From Oeiras to Saldanha takes 35 to 45 minutes by train with a metro transfer at Cais do Sodré. By car, it is slower at peak hours.

### Which are the cheapest areas in Oeiras?
Barcarena and the inland areas of Porto Salvo have T2 rents between €1,000 and €1,200, still significantly higher than most other municipalities.

### Is Oeiras safe?
Yes. It is consistently one of the municipalities with the lowest crime rate in the Lisbon Metropolitan Area.

### Which projects may change Oeiras in the coming years?
LIOS Ocidental is the structural long-term project. The continued expansion of the business parks and the arrival of new private residential developments will shape supply over the next 3 to 5 years.
