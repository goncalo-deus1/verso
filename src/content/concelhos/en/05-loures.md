# Living in Loures: what you need to know before choosing this area

**Quick summary:** Loures is one of the Lisbon Metropolitan Area municipalities with the greatest potential for appreciation in the coming years, due to the future Violet Line of the metro that will connect Loures to Odivelas. It has 202,000 residents and the median rent for a T2 (2-bedroom flat) is between €750 and €900, significantly cheaper than Lisboa, Cascais or Oeiras. A good choice for those who work in Parque das Nações or in eastern Lisboa. Less suitable for those who need to reach the western part of the capital.

*Updated on 30 April 2026.*

---

## Is Loures worth living in?

Loures combines three realities. The parishes closest to Lisboa (Camarate, Sacavém, Bobadela, Moscavide) are urban, dense and with a strong presence of immigrant communities. Loures-Vila and Santo António dos Cavaleiros are more residential, with a middle-class profile. The rural area (Bucelas, Fanhões, Lousa) has a more countryside-like character and attracts those looking for space.

For professionals who work in Lisboa Oriente or in Parque das Nações, Loures makes a lot of sense in terms of time and cost.

## How much does it cost to rent a T2 in Loures in 2026?

The median rent per square metre in Loures is between €9 and €10, according to INE data for Greater Lisbon. For an 80 m² T2, the rent is between €750 and €900 per month in the areas closest to Lisboa.

The most expensive areas are Sacavém, Moscavide and Santo António dos Cavaleiros. The most affordable are Bucelas, Fanhões and Apelação.

New developments in Santo António dos Cavaleiros (Élou Jardins and others, next to the future Torres da Bela Vista metro station) start at €280,000 for a T2 and €380,000 for a T3 on sale.

### Three-year T2 rent projection (2026 to 2029)

Loures is probably the Lisbon Metropolitan Area municipality with the greatest housing appreciation potential in the coming years, due to the entry into operation of the Violet Line of the metro. Base scenario: accumulated growth between 15% and 25% in the areas served by the new metro stations, bringing the median T2 rent to between €850 and €1,000 per month in 2029. In areas away from the stations (rural, inland), growth should be more moderate (8% to 12%).

## Population and density

Loures had 201,646 residents in the 2021 Census, 1.1% more than in 2011. The area is 167.24 km², with a density of 1,205 inhabitants per km².

Most of the population is concentrated in the southern parishes (Camarate, Sacavém, Bobadela, Moscavide, Portela), close to Lisboa. The rural parishes in the north are sparsely populated.

## Who lives in Loures? Resident profile

Loures has one of the most socially diverse profiles in the Lisbon Metropolitan Area. Cape Verdean, São Tomean, Brazilian and Ukrainian communities have a strong presence in Camarate, Apelação and Sacavém. The Portuguese middle class is concentrated in Santo António dos Cavaleiros, Loures-Vila and Bucelas. The old rural cores retain their own character (Bucelas is known for its wine).

The City Council runs active urban regeneration and affordable housing programmes, with a particular focus on Camarate and Apelação.

## Transport and connection to central Lisboa

The big change in Loures is the Violet Line of the metro.

**Violet Line of the metro** — `Ongoing`
11.5 km of light surface metro between Odivelas and Loures, with 17 stations (9 in Loures), serving Loures-Vila, Santo António dos Cavaleiros and Frielas. Awarded for €527 million via the Recovery and Resilience Plan; opening expected in 2026, but with confirmed delays and a European investigation into the subcontractor CRRC.

**LIOS Oriental** — `Under study`
Will connect Santa Apolónia to Sacavém, creating another fast transport axis on the north shore of the Tejo. It may reinforce the attractiveness of Sacavém and Bobadela.

The Red Line of the metro terminates in Moscavide, inside the municipality. The next stop is Humberto Delgado Airport (already in Lisboa-Olivais), which gives Loures direct access to two structural stations — one of its own, one on the border. The Yellow Line terminates in Odivelas, next to Loures.

By car, the A8 and the A1 are the main connections. Access to Lisboa is good off-peak (15 minutes to Lisboa Oriente), but traffic on the A1 and the 2ª Circular is difficult at peak hours.

## Planned urban projects in the municipality

The Violet Line will substantially change property values in Santo António dos Cavaleiros, Loures-Vila and Frielas.

Loures has around 1,000 families on the waiting list. There are several projects underway, including a recent tender for the acquisition of housing units with completion by March 2026.

The Metropolitan Plan to Support Disadvantaged Communities, financed by €121.5 million from the Recovery and Resilience Plan, has specific operations in Camarate.

There are more than a dozen private developments on sale, with strong concentration in Santo António dos Cavaleiros, Sacavém and Santa Iria de Azoia.

## Schools, healthcare and retail

In education, there is good coverage of public schools and private colleges in Loures-Vila. The universities are in Lisboa.

In healthcare, the Hospital Beatriz Ângelo (in Loures-Vila) is the public reference. The Hospital da Luz Lisboa Oriente (in Sacavém) and CUF Descobertas (in Lisboa, next to Moscavide) serve the area in the private sector.

In retail, Continente Loures, LoureShopping and Vasco da Gama (in Lisboa, but a few minutes from Sacavém and Moscavide) cover the offering.

## FAQ

### Is Loures good for families?
Santo António dos Cavaleiros, Loures-Vila and Bucelas are the most sought-after areas for families. They have schools, parks and a residential atmosphere. T3 rents are between €950 and €1,200.

### Is it worth buying a home in Loures before the Violet Line opens?
Most analysts believe so. The areas served by the future metro stations have significant appreciation potential. The risk is continued delays to operation.

### Loures or Odivelas: which is better?
Odivelas has a direct connection to the Lisboa metro via the Yellow Line, an advantage Loures will only have when the Violet Line opens. In exchange, Loures offers more space, a more residential atmosphere and lower prices.

### Which are the cheapest areas in Loures?
Apelação, Camarate (inland) and the rural areas (Lousa, Bucelas) have T2 rents below €700.

### Which areas will appreciate the most with the Violet Line?
The main stations planned in Loures-Vila, Santo António dos Cavaleiros and Frielas should see the greatest demand pressure.
