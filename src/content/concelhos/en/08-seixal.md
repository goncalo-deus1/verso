# Living in Seixal: what you need to know before choosing this area

**Quick summary:** Seixal is one of the best value-for-money municipalities in the Lisbon Metropolitan Area. It has 167,000 residents and a median T2 rent between €700 and €850. It has a direct ferry to Lisboa (Seixal-Cais do Sodré), Metro Sul do Tejo and Fertagus. A good choice for young families who want more space for less money and can tolerate 35 to 40 minutes to the centre of Lisboa. Less suitable for those who work in western Lisboa without using the Ponte 25 de Abril.

*Updated on 30 April 2026.*

---

## Is Seixal worth living in?

Seixal was one of the AML municipalities with the largest population growth in the last decade (+5.3% between 2011 and 2021), a sign that the cost-benefit ratio is good. The most sought-after parishes are Corroios (with Metro Sul do Tejo and Fertagus), Amora and Seixal-Vila (with ferry). Aldeia de Paio Pires and Fernão Ferro have a more rural profile and offer houses with land.

The combination of ferry, metro, train and proximity to Caparica beaches makes Seixal versatile. Pressure has been increasing in recent years.

## How much does it cost to rent a T2 in Seixal in 2026?

The median rent per square metre in Seixal is around €9 to €10, according to INE data for the Setúbal Peninsula. For an 80 m² T2, rent sits between €700 and €850 per month.

The most expensive areas are Corroios (with several metro and train stations) and Amora (next to the riverside zone). The more affordable ones are Aldeia de Paio Pires and Fernão Ferro.

### Three-year T2 rent projection (2026 to 2029)

Base scenario: accumulated growth between 12% and 18%, taking the median T2 rent to between €800 and €950 per month by 2029. Seixal benefits from the spillover effect from Almada and sustained population growth. The opening of the new airport in Alcochete (2034 or later) and the Third Tagus Crossing could increase the appeal of the south bank in the long term.

## Population and density

Seixal had 166,693 residents in the 2021 Census, 5.3% more than in 2011, one of the largest increases in the AML. The area is 95.50 km², which gives a density of 1,745 inhabitants per km².

The population is growing mainly in Corroios and Fernão Ferro. Aldeia de Paio Pires has also recorded an influx of new residents from Lisboa and Almada.

## Who lives in Seixal? Resident profile

Seixal has a working-class middle-class profile, with a strong industrial tradition. The population is mostly Portuguese, with significant Cape Verdean and Brazilian communities in Corroios and Amora.

Young families seek out Seixal for the more affordable prices and the combination of good transport with riverside quality of life. The tourist image of the Baía do Seixal and the tidal mills has also been attracting the creative middle class from Lisboa.

## Transport and connection to the centre of Lisboa

Seixal has three main options to Lisboa:

**Third Tagus Crossing** — `Announced`
New Lisboa-Chelas to Barreiro link with road and rail components, announced in 2024. The direct impact on Seixal is smaller than for Almada and Barreiro.

Ferry (Seixal-Cais do Sodré): about 25 minutes of crossing, with a 30-minute frequency.

Metro Sul do Tejo + Fertagus: the Metro Sul do Tejo passes through Corroios, with a Fertagus connection to Lisboa (Pragal-Roma-Areeiro-Sete Rios-Entrecampos).

Car via the Ponte 25 de Abril or Ponte Vasco da Gama: the Ponte 25 de Abril provides access to the western zone and centre of Lisboa. The Ponte Vasco da Gama provides access to Parque das Nações.

## Planned urban projects in the municipality

The Decree-Law of September 2025 transferred oversight of the former Arco Ribeirinho Sul projects (covering Almada, Barreiro and Seixal) to the new entity Parque Cidades do Tejo, S.A. There are plots to be developed in Seixal's riverside zone.

There are no publicly available official figures on families on the waiting list for Seixal (the municipality did not respond to Lusa's survey in July 2025), but there are PRR-funded projects under way.

There are several new developments on the market, especially in Corroios, Aldeia de Paio Pires and Fernão Ferro.

The refurbishment of the Baía do Seixal continues, with investment in public space.

## Schools, healthcare and retail

In education, public provision is good, with several schools of average quality. Higher education is in Almada (Universidade Nova) and Lisboa.

In healthcare, Hospital Garcia de Orta (in Almada) is the public reference and serves the entire south bank. There are health centres in the main parishes and private provision in smaller clinics.

In retail, Riosul Shopping (Seixal) is the main shopping centre. Local retail is strong in Corroios and Amora.

## FAQ

### Is Seixal good for families?
Very. Corroios, Amora and Aldeia de Paio Pires have good schools, parks and a calm residential atmosphere. T3 rent between €850 and €1,050.

### Is it worth living in Seixal if I work in central Lisboa?
Yes, if you like the ferry. From Seixal-Vila to Cais do Sodré is 25 minutes by ferry. From Corroios to Roma-Areeiro is about 30 minutes by metro plus train.

### Seixal or Almada: which is better?
Almada has a faster ferry (Cacilhas is closer), Almada Forum and direct proximity to Costa da Caparica. Seixal is cheaper, has more space and a less urban environment. For young families on a budget, Seixal. For more activity and proximity to Lisboa, Almada.

### What are the cheapest areas in Seixal?
Fernão Ferro and inland parts of Aldeia de Paio Pires have T2 rents below €700, with more space.

### What is the Baía do Seixal like?
Seixal's riverside front has been refurbished and has good environmental quality. The tidal mills and the old quay are tourist landmarks. It is one of the most beautiful and peaceful areas on the south bank.
