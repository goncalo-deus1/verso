# Living in Montijo: what you need to know before choosing this area

**Quick summary:** Montijo is one of the AML municipalities that has grown the most in population over the last decade (+8.8%), with 56,000 residents and a median T2 rent between €700 and €900. It has a direct ferry to Lisboa (Cais do Sodré) and is close to Parque das Nações via the Ponte Vasco da Gama. It is constrained by the preventive measures linked to the new Luís de Camões airport. A good choice for professionals working in eastern Lisboa who want an affordable price and proximity to the river. Less suited to those looking for areas with little planned urban change over the coming years.

*Updated on 30 April 2026.*

---

## Is Montijo worth living in?

Montijo combines three realities: the historic town with its traditional centre and ferry to Lisboa, the rural and agricultural parishes (Canha, Pegões, Atalaia) and the industrial-aeronautical area (Air Base No. 6). The municipality has attracted many new residents over the past ten years, drawn by price and quality of life.

The biggest unknown is the impact of the construction of the new Luís de Camões airport (part of the Canha territory falls within its boundaries) and of the preventive measures affecting land use within a 15 km radius.

## How much does it cost to rent a T2 in Montijo in 2026?

The median rent for a T2 in Montijo sits between €700 and €900 per month. The most expensive areas are Montijo-Vila (next to the ferry) and the residential areas west of the centre. The most affordable are Sarilhos Grandes, Canha and Pegões.

### Three-year T2 rent projection (2026 to 2029)

Base scenario: accumulated growth between 12% and 20%, taking the median T2 rent to between €800 and €1,000 per month by 2029. Montijo is subject to two opposing pressures. Upward pressure: from proximity to the new Luís de Camões airport, with the expectation of job creation and housing. Downward pressure: from the planning restrictions within the airport radius, which may limit new supply in some parishes.

## Population and density

Montijo had 55,732 residents in the 2021 Census, 8.8% more than in 2011, one of the largest increases in the AML. The area is 348.62 km², giving a density of 160 inhabitants per km². The real density is high in Montijo-Vila and very low in the rural parishes (Canha, Pegões).

## Who lives in Montijo? Resident profile

Montijo has a socially diverse profile. A significant share of the population is linked to Air Base No. 6 (military and civilian personnel). There are established working communities, plus young families arriving from Lisboa, Almada and Barreiro.

The international presence is low but has been growing thanks to the proximity of the new airport and the expectations tied to it.

## Transport and connection to the centre of Lisboa

Montijo has good options:

Ferry (Montijo–Cais do Sodré): around 25 minutes crossing time, with adequate frequency at peak times. It is the most common link to central Lisboa.

By car via the Ponte Vasco da Gama: access to Parque das Nações in 10 to 15 minutes. It is one of the fastest connections from the south bank to eastern Lisboa.

By car via the Ponte 25 de Abril: to western Lisboa and the city centre, around 30 to 40 minutes.

The Third Tagus Crossing, announced in 2024, will link Lisboa-Chelas to Barreiro with a rail component, but the direct impact on Montijo is smaller than for Almada and Barreiro.

## Planned urban projects in the municipality

**Luís de Camões airport** — `Announced`
New airport between Alcochete and Montijo (Canha), with completion scheduled for 2034–2037 and investment of around €9 billion. Part of the Montijo territory falls within the preventive measures of the 15 km radius, which limit planning operations in some areas.

Montijo has 176 families on the waiting list. A tender is being prepared for the allocation of 15 units, plus the construction of 91 new homes and the refurbishment of 100 existing ones, with completion scheduled for 2026.

There is moderate entry of private developments in Montijo-Vila and Afonsoeiro.

The riverfront refurbishment next to the ferry terminal is ongoing.

## Schools, healthcare and retail

In education, there is adequate public coverage and some private options in Montijo-Vila. There is no higher education locally.

In healthcare, the Hospital do Barreiro-Montijo is the public reference. The health centres in the parishes cover primary care.

In retail, the Freeport Outlet (in Alcochete but nearby) is the main shopping destination in the area. Traditional retail is strong in Montijo-Vila.

## FAQ

### Is Montijo good for families?
Yes. Montijo-Vila, Afonsoeiro and Atalaia have good schools and parks. T3 rent between €850 and €1,050.

### Is it worth living in Montijo if I work in eastern Lisboa?
Yes. It is one of the best south-bank options for Parque das Nações thanks to the Ponte Vasco da Gama (10 to 15 minutes).

### How does the new airport affect life in the municipality?
There are two effects. Positive: creation of skilled jobs and infrastructure investment. Negative: planning restrictions within the 15 km radius that limit construction in part of the territory, potential noise and a change in the landscape.

### Which are the cheapest areas in Montijo?
Sarilhos Grandes, Canha and Pegões have T2 rents below €700. Canha has a distinctly rural profile.

### Is it worth buying a home in Montijo now?
For horizons of 5 to 10 years, it makes sense. The airport's preventive measures are in force for two years (until 2027 or an extension) and may constrain new supply. Areas outside the 15 km radius may be more predictable.
